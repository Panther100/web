Admissions 2025–26

Begin Your Journey

Simple 4-step process to secure your seat. Applications reviewed on a rolling basis.

1
Fill Online Application
Complete the online form with your personal, academic, and program preferences. Upload required documents (10th & 12th marksheets, ID proof).
2
Entrance Test / Merit Review
Appear for BGC's own entrance test or submit your JEE / WBJEE / MAT / CAT / CMAT scores. Direct merit-based admissions available for top performers.
3
Counselling & Seat Allotment
Attend the online/offline counselling session. Seats allotted based on merit, category, and preference. Letter of Intent (LoI) issued.
4
Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay the admission fee via online portal. Collect your Student ID, timetable, and access to all campus portals. Welcome to BGC!
